About Smart Research Guide

Smart Research Guide was founded by Amankwaa Francis to help undergraduate students understand and complete their research projects with clarity, confidence, and academic excellence.

We believe research should not be confusing. Our goal is to guide students step by step from research topic selection to final project presentation.

Our Services

Research Proposal Support

Guidance on Chapter One, objectives, research questions, methodology, and proposal structure.

Questionnaire Design

Development of clear, objective-based questionnaires for academic research projects.

Data Analysis

Support with data cleaning, descriptive statistics, tables, charts, SPSS, Excel, and R analysis.

Thesis Writing Guidance

Step-by-step support for Chapters One to Five, including discussion, conclusion, and recommendations.
