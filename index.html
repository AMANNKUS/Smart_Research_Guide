About SRG

Research becomes manageable when the process is clear.

Smart Research Guide helps researchers and organisations move from uncertainty to a well-designed study, credible analysis and meaningful communication.

Founded by Amankwaa Francis, SRG provides focused guidance rather than one-size-fits-all answers. We listen to the research problem, identify the real gap and help each client build the skills and decisions needed to move forward confidently.

What we do

Specialist support across the research cycle.

Choose focused help for one stage or structured support across a complete project.

01

Research direction & proposals

Topic refinement, problem statements, objectives, research questions, literature direction and proposal structure.

02

Study design & research tools

Methodology guidance, sampling, questionnaires, interview guides and objective-to-method alignment.

03

Data management & analysis

Data cleaning, descriptive and inferential analysis, qualitative analysis, tables, charts and interpretation using SPSS, R and Excel.

04

Thesis & dissertation guidance

Chapter-by-chapter review, logical flow, discussion development, supervisor-comment response and final presentation.

05

Manuscripts & research communication

Journal manuscript preparation, abstracts, posters, slide decks, reports and research dissemination support.

06

NGO research & evaluation

Needs assessments, baseline and endline studies, monitoring and evaluation plans, data analysis and evidence-focused reporting.

07

Research training & capacity building

Practical workshops for students, research teams and organisations on methods, analysis, academic writing and evidence use.

Common questions

Before we begin.

Can SRG support only one part of my project?+

Yes. Support can focus on a single need—such as a questionnaire, analysis plan or discussion chapter—or follow a project through several milestones.

Do you work with research outside health sciences?+

Yes. SRG can assess projects across disciplines and will confirm whether the required subject and methodological expertise is available before work begins.

How is the cost determined?+

Pricing depends on the project stage, complexity, deliverables and timeline. After an initial discussion, you receive a clear scope and quotation.

Can NGOs request training for a team?+

Yes. Training can be tailored to programme teams, data collectors or research staff and delivered around practical organisational needs.
